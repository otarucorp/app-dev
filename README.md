# Dead Poets Society — My Favorite Movie

**_Directed by:_** Peter Weir  
**_Starring:_** Robin Williams, Ethan Hawke, Robert Sean Leonard  
**_Release:_** 1989 (classic coming-of-age drama)

---

## Quick synopsis
A new, unconventional English teacher, John Keating, encourages his all-boys preparatory school students to "seize the day" and think for themselves. His free-thinking teaching style awakens students to poetry, passion, and the idea of living life on their own terms — with beautiful, heartbreaking consequences.

---

## Why I love it
- **Powerful emotional beats** — balances humor, inspiration, and sadness.
- **Unforgettable performance** — Robin Williams brings warmth and intensity.
- **Themes that stick with you**:
  1. Individuality vs. conformity  
  2. The power of art and literature to change lives  
  3. The responsibility and cost of freedom
